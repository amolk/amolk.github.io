unwind shows your Claude Code and Codex sessions in one place. Sessions started by [callstack](/projects/callstack/) calls appear as a nested tree, with live status and cost on every branch. A monthly report totals tokens and cost across all your projects. Everything runs locally, and unwind sends no data anywhere.
